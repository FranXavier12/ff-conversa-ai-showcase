Data Deletion Instructions

FF Conversa AI

Last updated: November 18, 2026

1. Requesting Data Deletion

If you participated in authorized testing of FF Conversa AI and would like to request deletion of your personal information, please contact the project operator.

2. How to Submit a Request

Send an email to fjfigueroa.es@gmail.com with the subject:

FF Conversa AI - Data Deletion Request

Include the WhatsApp phone number associated with the test interaction, if applicable, and a brief description of your request.

Do not include passwords, medical records, identification documents, or other sensitive information in your initial request.

3. Request Review

We will review the request and may ask for limited additional information to verify that the requester is authorized to make it.

We will identify relevant information under our control and evaluate its deletion from applicable development and testing systems.

4. Deletion and Limitations

Where applicable and technically feasible, personal information under our control will be deleted in accordance with applicable requirements.

Some information may remain with independent third-party providers or may need to be retained to comply with legal obligations. We will explain relevant limitations when responding to the request.

5. Confirmation

We will respond to the request and communicate its outcome through the contact method provided. No fixed completion period is promised beyond any applicable legal requirement.
