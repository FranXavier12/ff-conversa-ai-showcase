Privacy Policy

FF Conversa AI

Last updated: November 18, 2026

1. About This Project

FF Conversa AI is an independent business messaging and automation project developed and operated by Francisco Figueroa. It is currently being developed and tested as a minimum viable product (MVP).

The current demonstration uses fictional business information and is not intended to provide live medical services or manage actual patient appointments.

2. Information We May Process

During authorized testing, the system may process WhatsApp phone numbers, display names, message contents, message identifiers, timestamps, and technical information required to operate and troubleshoot messaging workflows.

Test participants should not submit medical records, sensitive health information, payment card information, passwords, or other confidential information.

3. How Information Is Used

Information may be used to test message delivery and reception, develop automated responses, troubleshoot integrations, evaluate system functionality, and maintain basic operational records.

Information is not collected for advertising or sold to data brokers.

4. Third-Party Services

The project uses or may use Meta WhatsApp Business Platform, n8n, OpenAI API, and Google Workspace services such as Google Sheets. Information submitted through test workflows may be processed by the providers involved in those workflows, subject to their applicable terms and privacy policies.

Not every integration is active at every stage of development.

5. Data Security and Retention

We aim to limit access to project data and avoid exposing authentication credentials or private information in public repositories.

Because the project is still under development, security controls and data retention procedures are being evaluated. No absolute guarantee of security is made.

Test information may remain in workflow execution histories or connected services until reviewed and deleted, subject to provider capabilities and applicable requirements.

6. Data Sharing

Information may be transmitted to technology providers as necessary to operate authorized tests. We do not sell personal information. Information may also be disclosed where legally required.

7. Your Choices and Requests

You may request information about personal data associated with your participation in testing or request its deletion, subject to applicable legal obligations and technical limitations.

See our Data Deletion Instructions.

8. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated as the project evolves. Material changes to data processing practices should be reflected in an updated version.
